# PSU
In the context of banking APIs, "PSU" stands for "Payment Service User" or "Payment Service User Entity." The PSU refers to the end-user or the individual or entity that is authorized to initiate payment transactions or access account information through a payment service or banking application.

When using banking APIs, the PSU is the customer or account holder who grants consent to a third-party provider (TPP) to access their financial data or perform payment transactions on their behalf. The PSU's explicit consent is a fundamental requirement for any API-based interaction that involves accessing their sensitive financial information or initiating payments.

The European Union's Revised Payment Services Directive (PSD2) regulation includes provisions for the protection of PSUs and mandates the use of strong customer authentication (SCA) when accessing their accounts or initiating payments through APIs. This ensures that the PSU's financial data and transactions remain secure and under their control.
